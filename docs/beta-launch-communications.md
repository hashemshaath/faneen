## A. Internal Team Announcement

### Subject: Beta Launch — Qitaat Platform

**Team —**

We are opening the Qitaat beta to a small group of trusted providers and test customers.

**What this beta covers**

- Public pages, business profiles, and search
- Provider dashboard with leads, contracts, and analytics
- Contract creation from leads, BOQ, execution sites, PDF export
- Admin contract and provider analytics

**What is NOT in this beta**

- Custom payment collection (payment schedules are tracked, not collected)
- Visual regression baselines (requires Docker reseed outside Lovable)
- Full admin drilldowns beyond contracts
- Custom formula pricing

**Known risks**

- Manual PDF visual QA is required for Arabic layout accuracy
- Visual regression is manual until baselines are reseeded
- Some admin drilldowns are deferred

**Support escalation**

- First response: Support owner (triage)
- Technical issues: Technical owner
- Privacy/security: Product owner + immediate pause
- PDF or contract failures: QA owner

**Daily reporting**

Each day of the beta, the admin on call will fill the Daily Beta Report (see Section H). Report is due by 10:00 AM the following day.

**Success targets**

- 3+ providers fully onboarded
- 5+ leads submitted
- 2+ contracts created from leads
- 2+ PDFs exported and verified
- Zero critical privacy or security issues

## B. Provider Invitation — Arabic

---

**عنوان: دعوة للانضمام إلى المرحلة التجريبية لمنصة قِطاعات**

مرحباً،

نود دعوتكم للانضمام إلى المرحلة التجريبية لمنصة **قِطاعات** — الدليل الصناعي المتخصص لقطاعات الألمنيوم، الزجاج، الخشب، الحديد، والصناعات المساندة.

**ما هي قِطاعات؟**

قِطاعات منصة صناعية تربط مزودي الخدمات والمنتجات بالعملاء. نساعدكم في عرض أعمالكم، استقبال طلبات العملاء، وإدارة العقود — كل ذلك من لوحة تحكم واحدة.

**ما يمكنكم تجربته في هذه المرحلة التجريبية:**

1. **الملف التعريفي لعملكم**
   - إنشاء وتحرير ملفكم التعريفي
   - إضافة الصور، الخدمات، والفروع
   - عرض شارة العضوية والتقييمات

2. **استقبال الطلبات (الفرص)**
   - استلام طلبات العملاء مباشرة
   - متابعة حالة كل طلب
   - تحويل الطلب إلى عقد

3. **العقود**
   - إنشاء عقود من الطلبات الواردة
   - إضافة بنود الكميات (BOQ)
   - تحديد مواقع التنفيذ
   - إرسال العقد للعميل لمراجعته وقبوله

4. **تصدير PDF**
   - تصدير العقود بصيغة PDF باللغة العربية
   - QR code للتحقق من صحة العقد
   - طباعة احترافية

5. **التحليلات**
   - متابعة أداء العقود والإيرادات
   - تقارير فترية (أسبوعية، شهرية، سنوية)
   - مقارنة الأداء

**ما نتوقعه منكم:**

- تجربة الميزات المذكورة أعلاه بشكل حقيقي
- الإبلاغ عن أي أخطاء أو صعوبات تواجهكم
- تقديم ملاحظاتكم بصراحة — نحتاج لمعرفة ما يعمل وما يحتاج تحسيناً

**كيف تُرسلون الملاحظات:**

يمكنكم استخدام نموذج الملاحظات المرفق (رابط النموذج) أو التواصل مباشرة مع فريق الدعم.

**تنويه:** هذه مرحلة تجريبية. بعض الميزات قيد التطوير وقد تواجهون تجربة غير نهائية. نقدّر صبركم وتعاونكم.

## C. Provider Invitation — English

---

**Subject: Invitation to Join the Qitaat Beta**

Hello,

You are invited to join the **Qitaat** beta — the specialized industrial directory serving aluminum, glass, wood, steel, and allied sectors.

**What is Qitaat?**

Qitaat is an industrial platform that connects providers with customers. We help you showcase your business, receive customer requests, and manage contracts — all from a single dashboard.

**What you can test in this beta:**

1. **Business Profile**
   - Create and edit your business profile
   - Add images, services, and branches
   - Display membership badges and reviews

2. **Receiving Leads**
   - Receive customer requests directly
   - Track the status of each request
   - Convert a lead into a contract

3. **Contracts**
   - Create contracts from incoming leads
   - Add bill-of-quantity line items (BOQ)
   - Set execution sites
   - Send contracts to clients for review and acceptance

4. **PDF Export**
   - Export contracts as Arabic PDFs
   - QR code for contract verification
   - Print-ready professional output

5. **Analytics**
   - Monitor contract performance and revenue
   - Periodic reports (weekly, monthly, yearly)
   - Performance comparisons

**What we expect from you:**

- Test the features above in real scenarios
- Report any bugs or difficulties you encounter
- Provide honest feedback — we need to know what works and what needs improvement

**How to send feedback:**

Use the attached feedback form (form link) or contact our support team directly.

**Disclaimer:** This is a beta phase. Some features are under development and you may experience non-final behavior. We appreciate your patience and cooperation.

## I. Tone Guidelines

### For all beta communications

1. **Clear and simple**
   - Avoid jargon. If a technical term is necessary, explain it.
   - Use short sentences. Arabic sentences should not exceed 25 words.

2. **Professional but human**
   - No corporate speak. Write as if speaking to a colleague.
   - Use "we" and "you" — not passive voice.

3. **Honest beta language**
   - Never claim the product is "finished" or "perfect."
   - Use phrases like: "مرحلة تجريبية," "beta phase," "قيد التطوير," "under development."
   - Always include the beta disclaimer.

4. **No exaggerated claims**
   - Do not promise revenue, traffic, or conversion numbers.
   - Do not claim "the best" or "the first" without evidence.
   - Focus on what the platform does, not what it guarantees.

5. **Arabic-first**
   - Arabic is the primary language for provider and customer messages.
   - English is secondary, for bilingual audiences.
   - Ensure Arabic text is natural and culturally appropriate (not machine-translated tone).

6. **No unsupported statistics**
   - Do not include user counts, revenue figures, or growth percentages unless they are verified and sourced.
   - Beta metrics should be reported as "so far" or "to date," not projections.

7. **Privacy-first**
   - Always mention data protection in customer-facing messages.
   - Never imply that user data is shared broadly.

8. **Action-oriented**
   - Every message should end with a clear next step.
   - The feedback form link should be included in every external communication.
